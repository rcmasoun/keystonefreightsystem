About Keystone

The keystone between shippers and carriers.

Edmonton-based, North America connected — here's who we are and how we work.

Our Story

A brokerage named after the piece that holds everything up.

In an arch, the keystone is the wedge-shaped stone at the very top that locks every other piece in place — remove it, and the structure falls. That's the position Keystone Freight Systems takes between shippers and carriers.

Based in Edmonton, Alberta, we built our brokerage around a simple standard: know the lane, know the carrier, and stay on the shipment until it's delivered. No freight gets handed off and forgotten.

Whether it's a single LTL pallet or a full cross-border truckload, our team treats every load with the same attention — because to the customer waiting on it, every load is the important one.

"Strength. Service. Solutions."

Our guiding standard for every shipment, every carrier relationship, every quote.
